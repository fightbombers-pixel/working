## 6. ДЕ ЛЕЖАТЬ «ЧЕКИ» · 7:20–9:30

**[HOST]**
In the US, a charity has to file a public tax form every year. It's called Form 990. Revenue, salaries, how much goes to programs versus overhead. Anyone can read it.
*[СКРІН: ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, WaterAid America, EIN 30-0181674]*

**[VO]** *[ГРАФІКА: стовпці доходу WaterAid America FY2022–FY2025]*
WaterAid America normally raises around eighteen to twenty-six million dollars a year. In its last filed year, eighteen point five million.
TeamWater alone is forty-one point eight. About two and a third times their normal year.
*[ГРАФІКА: пунктирний стовпець FY2026 «+$41.8M?»]*

**[HOST]**
So the 990 for the TeamWater year should be the most interesting document this charity has ever filed. Where is it?
*[ГРАФІКА: календар — фінрік 1.04.2025–31.03.2026 → строк 15.08.2026 → з продовженням 15.02.2027]*
Their financial year runs April to March. The TeamWater money landed in the year that ended March 2026. With the standard extension, the deadline is **February 15th, 2027**. And last year they used that extension too.
So the fact that it's not out yet? Completely normal. Not a red flag.

**[HOST]** *(нахиляється до камери)*

*[СКРІН: Schedule F, FY2025: «SUB-SAHARAN AFRICA… 1,313,223 WIRE TRANSFER»]*
This is how WaterAid America reports money it sends abroad: by *region*. «Sub-Saharan Africa: 1.3 million.» Not «Lagos pipeline: X dollars».
So the most detailed public document this system produces will still not show where each dollar went.

**[VO]** *[ГРАФІКА: горизонтальні смуги «програми / управління / фандрейзинг»]*
And one more number people will want to throw around. In its last filed year, WaterAid America spent about sixty-five percent on programs and thirty-five percent on management and fundraising. The year before, the overhead was closer to twenty-six.
*[ГРАФІКА: дисклеймер «NOT the TeamWater split»]*
